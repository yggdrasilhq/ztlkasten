# Field methods

**Transect direction changed in 2031.** Counts before and after are not
comparable, and any tally that mixes them is wrong in a way that looks fine.

**Zeroes are recorded.** A notebook that only holds sightings overstates every
season it describes.

**Testimony is kept even when unprovable.** Marked as testimony, never promoted
to a record. The pattern only appears if the weak entries are kept too.
