# Nightjar at dusk

Churring from the heath edge, twenty minutes after sunset. First of the year and
three weeks earlier than the book says it should be.

No photograph, so this is testimony and nothing more — recorded here because an
entry I cannot prove is still an entry, and the pattern only shows up if I keep
the unprovable ones too.

[[wren-halloway]] heard it as well, which is a second witness and not a
recording.
